Paris Overview

Paris, the capital of France, is globally renowned for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. Known as the "City of Light," Paris has been a major European city for over two millennia.

With iconic landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and the Louvre Museum, Paris attracts millions of visitors each year. The city's elegant architecture, world-class museums, and romantic atmosphere make it one of the most beloved destinations in the world.

Famous Attractions

Eiffel Tower

The iconic iron lattice tower and symbol of Paris, offering breathtaking views of the city.

Louvre Museum

The world's largest art museum, home to the Mona Lisa and thousands of masterpieces.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Gothic architectural marvel and one of the finest examples of French Gothic architecture.

Art & Culture

Paris has been the cultural heart of Europe for centuries, influencing art, literature, and philosophy worldwide. From the bohemian quarters of Montmartre to the sophisticated galleries of Saint-Germain, the city breathes creativity.

Museums

Over 130 museums including the Louvre, Musée d'Orsay, and Centre Pompidou.

Architecture

From medieval Gothic to modern glass pyramids, Paris showcases architectural evolution.

Literature

Home to countless writers, poets, and the famous Shakespeare and Company bookstore.

French Cuisine

Experience the finest culinary traditions in the world, from street-side crêperies to Michelin-starred restaurants.

Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit

April to June and October to early November offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds.

Getting Around

The Metro system is efficient and connects all major attractions. Consider a weekly pass.

Language

While French is preferred, most tourist areas have English-speaking staff.

Currency

Euro (€) is the official currency. Credit cards are widely accepted.
